C — Modern Clean / Conversion-First

White + green palette, urgency triggers (limited seats, open admissions), clear benefit bullets, trust badges. Designed purely to get enquiries. Uses scarcity, clarity, and friction-reduction psychology. Best for maximizing admission conversions.

Sales Psychology Behind Each
A — Authority Model
Cialdini's Authority + Social Proof. Parents trust institutions that look established and show numbers.
B — Emotional Model
Kahneman's System 1 thinking. Parents make school decisions emotionally, justify rationally later.
C — Conversion Model
Fogg Behavior Model. Reduce friction + add motivation + trigger = enquiry. Pure CRO approach.
